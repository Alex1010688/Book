Appendix A — Glossary

Term Meaning in this course
Approach control Control of signal clearance using specified approach conditions, for example to support a speed reduction before a divergence
Approach locking Locking that preserves route protection after a signal has cleared and is then replaced, until the specified release conditions are met
Aspect The indication displayed by a signal
Axle counter A system that determines section occupation using axle passage at defined detection points and a valid evaluator state
Bi-directional line A line signalled for movements in both directions
Ballast resistance The electrical resistance associated with leakage between rails through the track support and surroundings
Bearer The transmission path or service carrying a communications channel
Bond An electrical connection providing the required continuity between rails or around a joint
Call-on A movement into an occupied line under specified signalling conditions
Cess The area alongside the outside of the running lines
Clear indication An indication that a section meets the system’s clear-state conditions
Control table A record of the functional requirements for a signalling route, point or other item
Crossover Two point ends arranged to connect adjacent tracks
Dead locking Point locking associated with occupation of the relevant train-detection section
Detection Evidence that the specified equipment conditions have been established
Diamond crossing A same-level crossing of two tracks, without necessarily providing a connection between them
DLM Data Link Module, an interface to an SSI data-link path
ELR Engineers’ Line Reference, a code identifying a railway line or section for location records
Facing movement A movement approaching point toes and the divergence
Fail-safe Designed so that specified failures do not produce an unsafe permission
FDM Frequency Division Multiplex, sharing a path using distinct frequency channels
Flank protection Protection of a route from an unwanted movement approaching from outside its direct line of travel
Four-foot Conventional name for the space between a track’s running rails
Front contact A relay contact closed in its operated state in the basic relay description
Back contact A relay contact closed in its released state in the basic relay description
Gauge In the trackwork chapter, the specified distance between the inner faces of the running rails
Ground frame Local controls for a limited group of equipment, with a release and protective arrangements where required
Headway Separation of successive trains, expressed as distance or time under stated conditions
Insulated rail joint A rail joint designed to provide electrical separation
Interlocking Equipment and logic enforcing the specified relationships between routes, points, signals and other conditions
LDT Long Distance Terminal, adapting an SSI link to an appropriate telecommunications bearer
Location case A trackside housing for signalling interfaces, terminals, supplies and other local apparatus
MPM Multiprocessor Module in the SSI architecture described
Normal/reverse The two designated point positions recorded in the scheme information
NX Entrance-exit route setting
Overlap A defined protected distance or arrangement beyond a stop signal
PCM Pulse Code Modulation, used for digital voice transmission
PPM Panel Processor Module, interfacing SSI with the signaller’s control equipment
Right-side failure A failure producing a more restrictive result than the traffic conditions require
Route locking Locking that preserves the required route and protection until its release conditions are met
Sectional release Progressive release of route locking behind a train under the specified sequence
Shunt In train detection, a conductive connection between rails; in operations, a category of ancillary train movement
Six-foot Conventional name for the space between adjacent tracks
SSI In this book, the British Rail-developed Solid State Interlocking system
Stick circuit A relay holding circuit using a contact of the operated relay to maintain its energisation
Stock rail A fixed rail against which a switch blade closes
Subsidiary signal A signal associated with a main signal and used for a specified movement while the main aspect remains at danger
Switch blade A moving rail that selects the path through a set of points
Tail cable A local cable between an equipment housing and a trackside item or rail connection
TDM Time Division Multiplex, sharing a path using a timed sequence of channels
Ten-foot Conventional name for the wider space between pairs of tracks in a four-track arrangement
TFM Trackside Functional Module, an SSI interface to local inputs and outputs
TORR Train Operated Route Release, using a defined train-passage sequence
Track circuit Train detection using rails as part of an electrical circuit
Trailing movement A movement approaching points from a converging track towards the common end
Up/Down Established railway direction designations, identified from the applicable local records
Wrong-side failure A failure producing a less restrictive result than the conditions justify

A.1 Further electrical and equipment terms

Term Meaning in this course
ACE Axle Counter Evaluator in the AZLM system
Ampere-hour (Ah) A unit of electric charge used for battery capacity under specified discharge conditions
AZLM A multi-section digital axle-counter system
Back drive A mechanism providing controlled movement farther along a switch rail
Ballast leakage Current taking a path between rails through their support and surroundings
Bonding diagram A drawing showing individual rail connections, insulation and bonding arrangements
Core An individual insulated conductor within a cable
Drop-shunt value The specified shunt resistance that just causes the occupied response under stated test conditions
EAK Trackside electronic unit associated with an axle-counter detection point
Earth-free circuit A circuit arranged without an intentional connection of its working conductors to earth
Electrolyte The ion-conducting medium in an electrical cell
Escapement A mechanism coordinating phases of movement, such as unlocking, driving and relocking points
Float charging Maintaining a rechargeable battery at its specified standby charging condition while supporting the load
Internal resistance A source characteristic contributing to voltage fall under signalling load
OSS Overspeed sensor system within TPWS
Phantom aspect Apparent signal illumination caused by extraneous light rather than the intended display
Rectifier A device or circuit converting alternating current into unidirectional current
Residual pin A non-magnetic part maintaining a small gap in a relay’s attracted magnetic path
Roundel A coloured optical element moved to select the displayed aspect in a searchlight signal
Secondary cell A rechargeable electrical cell
Sheath The outer protective covering of a cable
Snubbing Controlled electrical braking of a motor near the end of its movement in the arrangement described
Specific gravity Density relative to a reference, used for electrolyte assessment where appropriate
TPWS Train Protection and Warning System
Track-circuit interrupter A sacrificial element that interrupts a circuit during a defined irregular wheel movement
Transformer Equipment providing the designed a.c. voltage conversion and isolation in a signalling supply
TSS Train stop system within TPWS

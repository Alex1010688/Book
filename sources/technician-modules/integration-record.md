All seven contents PDFs were also checked. Representative original technical illustrations were visually checked for circuit tracing, track-circuit leakage, point-machine sequence, power conversion and cable continuity. Additional diagrams are newly drawn conceptual figures.

Numeric maintenance limits and test connection sequences have not been promoted into generic instructions. Explanations distinguish the functions and evidence, referring practical procedures to the applicable controlled documentation. ATP and HABD remain excluded from the book. TPWS is incorporated from Signals.pdf within the existing lineside-signals chapter.

The power module uses nickel-iron and nickel-cadmium terminology inconsistently. The new chapter distinguishes the chemistries and avoids its conflated plate description.
